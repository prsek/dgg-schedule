Notes

  • Movies: Mon–Thu: Not allowed. Friday: Start at 22:00 or 1 hr after Destiny’s stream ends, end at 12:00. Poll after 2 films to step down if >50% vote yes. Saturday: No movies, only TV. Sunday: One movie after TV episodes (post-08:00), paused until midnight if series runs late.
  • TV/Series: Only at Saturday (22:00, 2 episodes, finished series) and Sunday (08:00, 2 episodes, current series). Banned outside these slots.
  • Games/Radio: Allowed any time, 1 hr limit, 30–45 min gap after conducting. Interaction-heavy streams face stricter moderation.
  • Flaired Conductors: Priority for slots; non-flaired need mod permission (tena, rin_lux, RTBA, or flaired conductor).
  • Destiny Streaming: Overrides schedule; conducting stops immediately, no makeup unless specified.
  • Vacation Rules: If Destiny gone >2 days, flaired conductors stream one movie daily (11:00–22:00). Same for 1–2 days with notice.
  • Bans: Illegal conducting: 3-day IP ban, exponential increase for repeats. Spamming or bad behavior (e.g., harassment) banned. Illegal stream links banned.
  • Moderation: Enforces rules to prevent chat takeover. Unbanning requires supermajority; no third chances.
